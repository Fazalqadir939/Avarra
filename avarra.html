Where things stand

Avarra is being built solo, in public phases. Here's what's real so far.

A kingdom that runs while you're offline

Everything in Avarra is built around one constraint: it has to work with no connection at all. That shaped the whole game, not just the tech.

Auto-gather economy

One tap sends every idle villager to the nearest wood, stone, or food source. Less time managing menus, more time managing a war.

AI kingdoms, not bots on rails

Up to three opposing kingdoms build, expand, and attack on their own state-driven logic — economy, then military, then war. Difficulty comes from how fast they grow, not scripted cheating.

25+ hand-built battlegrounds

Maps rebuilt from real ancient-warfare terrain — river crossings, hill forts, siege grounds — each one playable start to finish, offline.

Built for a thumb, not a mouse

Drag to select an army, double-tap to call in every soldier you own, hold for a quick-build wheel. Controls designed around a phone screen from day one.
