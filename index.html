What we're building toward

Velaxor is a solo-founded studio right now — every design and technical decision starts from the same question: does this still work with no signal at all?

Offline by design

No always-on servers, no forced logins. Games that work the same on a plane as they do at home.

Built for touch

Controls designed around a phone from the start, not a desktop game shrunk down to fit a smaller screen.

Fair, not predatory

Monetization that speeds things up if you want it to — never a wall that decides whether you can keep playing.
